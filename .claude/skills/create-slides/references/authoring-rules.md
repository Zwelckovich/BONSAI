# Authoring rules

Keynote-clean means restraint: one idea, one accent, one visual, generous air. Every rule here has a reason; the validator or the audience catches the violation.

## Writing

1. **The headline is the claim.** A complete sentence someone could quote — "AlexNet didn't win. It demolished." — never a label ("Results"). The eyebrow is the label.
2. **One idea per slide.** If a slide needs "and", split it.
3. **Lead with the punchline;** the supporting line explains, it does not tease.
4. **Body text ≥ 40 px, captions ≥ 32 px.** The template sizes already comply; do not go below `.small`.
5. **Max six lines of body copy**, max four rows, max four cards. Cut words before shrinking.
6. **Numbers get their own slide** (`.stat`) or their own chart. Never bury a number in a paragraph.
7. **Notes: 2–4 sentences** the presenter can say, drawn from the transcript passage (quote the anecdote or number that makes the slide land). Notes are for the speaker; the slide is for the room.

## Layout catalogue (deck.css)

```html
<div class="slide center">  eyebrow · h1 · .lede                       <!-- cover, divider, single claim -->
<div class="slide">         eyebrow · h2 · .list/.row | .stat | .quote <!-- default -->
<div class="slide top">     eyebrow · h2 · .figure (pattern)           <!-- figure needs height -->
<div class="slide split">   <div>eyebrow · h2 · .lede</div> · .figure  <!-- text left, figure right -->
<div class="cols cols-3">   .card × 3 (icon · h3 · p)                  <!-- comparison by hand -->
<div class="list">          .row (.num · text) × ≤ 4                    <!-- sequence, definition -->
<div class="stat">          .num · .label                                <!-- one number -->
<blockquote class="quote">  text · <cite>                               <!-- quote -->
<div class="kicker">        bottom-left caption (source, date)
```

Icons: `<svg class="icon"><use href="assets/icons.svg#i-brain"/></svg>` — 83 Lucide symbols; list them with `grep -o 'id="i-[^"]*"' composition/assets/icons.svg`. One icon per card at most; icons never replace a word in a sentence.

Per-deck styling goes in the `<style>` block of `composition/index.html` (accent, a special cover). Never edit `deck.css` inside a deck — it is the shared design system.

## Motion

- `data-anim` on the eyebrow, headline, lede: they stagger in on entry. Not on figures (patterns handle themselves).
- **Progressive reveal by default** for rows, cards, and every pattern: `data-fragment="1..n"`. Fragments are the presenter's pacing — one thought per click. Three fragments is the sweet spot; more than five is a sign the slide should split.
- A reveal never *moves* what is already on screen. Reserve space; do not reflow.
- Effects: rows `rise` (default), cards `rise`, numbers `pop`, strokes `draw`, bars `grow`. Do not mix effects within one slide.
- Static slides declare no fragments. A slide with fragments must show something new on every click (validate.mjs and deck.js warn on mismatches).

## Time arithmetic when inserting or removing slides

Slots are `10 × index`. To insert a slide at position k: shift `data-start` of every later scene by +10, shift their fragment times by +10, bump `#root[data-duration]` by 10, then rewrite the island (the wrapper copy is regenerated by `bun run check`). Removing is the inverse. Do it with one careful pass, then run `bun run check` — lint catches fragment-out-of-range and overlaps immediately.

## Media

- Videos: `<video id="clip-1" class="clip" data-start="…" data-duration="10" src="assets/intro.mp4" muted playsinline>` inside the scene, plus `"autoplay": true` on that slide in the island. Re-encode anything over ~20 MB (`ffmpeg -i in.mp4 -vcodec libx264 -crf 24 -an out.mp4`).
- Images: `<img>` with explicit width/height inside `.figure`; keep them under 2 MB.
- Ambient loop: see `themes.md`.

## Apple-clean do / don't

**Do:** one accent per deck · white (or near-black) type · tabular numbers · balanced headlines (`text-wrap: balance` is on) · soft glow, no gradients on text · hairlines, not boxes.
**Don't:** drop shadows on text · more than two font sizes on a slide besides eyebrow/kicker · emoji · decorative icons scattered around · centered body paragraphs · full-bleed stock photos with text on top.
